Is the eye test really free?

Yes. Our complimentary eye test is available at all open Sankhla Opticals stores. You are welcome to book whether or not you are buying eyewear.

Do I need to sign in to shop?

You can browse and add favourites to your bag without an account. Sign in or create an account only when you are ready to place an order.

Can I try a frame before I buy?

Of course. Choose your nearest store at the top of the page and visit us to try a frame and get a complimentary fit adjustment.

Can I order contact lenses online?

Yes. To keep your eyes comfortable and safe, we will confirm your valid prescription before fulfilling a contact-lens order.
